# Licences and source attribution

## Freesound audio

Every distributed Freesound WAV retains the licence associated with its source recording. This release does not assign a blanket new licence to the original audio.

- **CC0 1.0:** https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- **CC BY 4.0:** https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Consult `metadata/freesound_attribution.csv` for each original title, creator, source URL, licence URL, original source description, and modification notice. The descriptions preserve supplied source notes, including any additional attribution information. Licence metadata is the stored collection-time record; `license_observed_at` records its observation time and is not a claim of a fresh source-site check.

For CC BY 4.0 material, provide appropriate credit, link to the licence, and indicate modifications. Preserve applicable attribution supplied by the original creator. Consult the linked licence for its complete terms.

All supplied audio was preprocessed as described in `README.md`; it is not presented as a newly recorded original work by the dataset curators.

## DPK audio

DPK audio is not distributed in this release. `metadata/dpk_sources.csv` supplies our class labels and recorded source paths. Obtain original recordings from [Drum and Percussion Kits](https://zenodo.org/records/3994999) and consult the original source terms. The Freesound licence entries do not apply to DPK audio.
